Delete your CredEasy account

Use the in-app option to delete your CredEasy account and associated cloud data. This page explains the steps and what is or is not removed.

Last updated October 9, 2026

1. Request deletion

  1. Open CredEasy and sign in to the Google account associated with your CredEasy account.
  2. Open Settings, choose Delete account, and review the confirmation carefully.
  3. Enter your Security PIN to confirm. The app will report whether the deletion succeeded. If it reports an error, your local data is not cleared; contact support for help.

If you cannot access the app, email help.credeasy@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Google sign-in. Ask for a CredEasy account deletion and include the account email address. Do not include your password, Security PIN, or authentication tokens. We may ask for information needed to verify the request.

2. What is deleted

When an in-app account deletion completes successfully, CredEasy deletes the account from its cloud authentication service, removes associated CredEasy cloud records and private profile/party media, clears ledger data stored by the app on the device where you requested deletion, and signs that device out. CredEasy does not intentionally retain the deleted account profile or ledger data after successful deletion.

Offline-only use does not create a CredEasy sign-in account. Offline records are stored on the device and cannot be remotely deleted through an account deletion request.

3. What remains

  • Google Drive backups: Account deletion does not remove backup files already stored in your Google Drive. Delete those files from Drive separately.
  • Other devices and offline copies: Local records or cached copies on another device are not remotely cleared by deleting the account. Remove those copies on each device, or uninstall CredEasy and clear its app storage.
  • Third-party records: Google and other service providers may retain information under their own policies. Their deletion controls and retention periods are managed by those providers.

4. Need help?

For account deletion questions, contact help.credeasy@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for more information about data handling.
