FAQ

Quick answers

Does CredEasy work offline?

Yes. Every entry writes to your phone first. When you're back online, CredEasy syncs to the cloud automatically. You can use the app for a full day with no signal.

Do I need a smart phone?

Android 8.0 or above. Any handset from the last five years (₹6,000+) will work smoothly. We're working on an iOS version — join the waitlist in the app.

Can my family use it with me?

Yes. Invite up to 5 people as staff from Settings. Speaker recognition auto-tags the right person on each entry — no login switching.

What if my Hindi isn't pure?

Good. Speak how you speak. CredEasy handles Hinglish and regional code-switching by design. The voice model was trained on real shopkeeper speech, not textbook Hindi.

Will there be ads?

Only on the ₹29 Basic plan. Every paid plan is ad-free — forever. Our business model is your subscription, not advertisers.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your ledger is stored on your device and in our cloud backup. Phone lost or broken? Sign in on a new device and your data is right there. We never sell or share your data.

How do I cancel?

From your Google Play or App Store account → Subscriptions. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. We don't bury the cancel button.
