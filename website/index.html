A few clear answers.

Choose the setup that fits how you want to store and back up your business records.

Does CredEasy work offline?

You can choose local-only offline mode. In that mode, your ledger stays in local app storage and cloud sync and Drive backup are disabled.

How do Google Drive backups work?

Sign in with Google and grant the Drive permission requested by the app. Backup and restore are available through the Google Drive Backup screen.

What can I manage in CredEasy?

CredEasy includes parties and transaction history, optional inventory, billing and reports. The exact features you use depend on your business.

Is there a subscription?

No. CredEasy is free to use and supported by ads. There is no subscription or trial period.
