1. Who we are

CredEasy is a mobile business ledger for small businesses. It helps users keep customer and supplier records, transactions, inventory, bills and reports together.

In this policy, “CredEasy,” “we,” and “us” refer to the CredEasy service and its operators.

2. Information handled by the app

Business and ledger information

When you use the app, you may enter business profile details such as a business name, phone number, address, email, GSTIN, UPI ID, business category, profile image or payment QR image. You may also add customer and supplier names, phone numbers, photos, opening balances, transaction dates, amounts and notes, along with inventory and bill information.

Account information

If you continue with Google, Google provides account details such as your name, email address and profile image for sign-in. CredEasy does not receive your Google password. The app also requests Google Drive file access to support its backup and restore feature.

Voice assistant

If you choose to use the voice assistant, your audio is sent to CredEasy’s backend for transcription and response generation. The resulting request text is processed to provide an answer. Voice requests and transcripts are not retained by CredEasy beyond the active request/session, though the providers processing a request may handle it under their own policies.

Diagnostics and advertising

The app may process device and usage information needed for diagnostics, app operation and advertising. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction information under Google’s policies.

3. How we use information

  • To show and manage the business profiles, parties, transactions, inventory, bills and reports you create.
  • To authenticate your account and, when authorized, back up or restore business data using Google Drive.
  • To synchronize signed-in account data between devices where cloud sync is available. Cloud sync may be delayed or unavailable; your local records remain the app’s primary working copy.
  • To transcribe and answer voice-assistant requests that you choose to submit.
  • To support the free app with advertisements and to diagnose reliability issues.

Business records can include other people’s information. You are responsible for having the authority and any required permission to enter and use customer, supplier, employee or other personal information in CredEasy.

4. Storage, sync and backups

  • On your device: The app stores ledger data locally so it can be used on that device, including in offline-only mode.
  • Google Drive: Drive backup is available only after Google sign-in and Drive permission are granted. Backups are stored in the Google account’s Drive and can be used for restore. A backup cannot recover information that was never successfully uploaded.
  • Cloud sync: Signed-in accounts may use Supabase for authentication and cross-device sync. Sync depends on network, service availability and successful writes; it is not a substitute for checking that a Drive backup completed.
  • Device backup: Android’s operating-system backup may include app data if device backup is enabled and succeeds. This is controlled by the device/platform and is not guaranteed.

Records and images may remain on a device or in a previously created Drive backup until you remove them from those locations or replace/delete that backup.

5. Service providers

CredEasy relies on third-party services to provide some app features. Depending on which features you use, relevant information may be processed by:

  • Google: account authentication, Google Drive backup/restore and Google AdMob advertising. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: authentication and cloud data storage/sync. See Supabase Privacy.
  • CredEasy’s backend and configured AI/speech providers: voice requests you submit for transcription and response generation. See OpenAI’s Privacy Policy when an OpenAI service processes a request.

Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Their availability, retention practices and controls are not operated by CredEasy.

6. Your choices and requests

  • You can choose offline-only use; in that mode, app records remain local and Google Drive backup and cloud sync are not used.
  • You can choose whether to use optional features such as the voice assistant, and you can revoke Google permissions through your Google Account settings.
  • You can review and export records using the app’s available export features. You control information you enter into your ledger.
  • For a privacy question or to request help with account-associated data, contact help.credeasy@gmail.com. Include enough detail for us to understand your request, but do not email passwords or authentication tokens.

Removing the app or clearing its storage can remove local data from that device. It does not automatically remove copies already stored in Google Drive or data retained by third-party providers.

7. Children’s privacy

CredEasy is intended for business users and is not designed for children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will revise the date above when we publish an update. Where required, we will provide notice through the app or another appropriate channel.

9. Contact us

Questions or privacy requests: help.credeasy@gmail.com.
